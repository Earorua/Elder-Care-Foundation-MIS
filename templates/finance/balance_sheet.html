Asset Composition
Notes to Financial Statements

These financial statements have been prepared on the cash basis of accounting. Assets and liabilities are recognized when cash transactions occur.

Cash and cash equivalents represent the cumulative net cash position: total cash received from all revenue sources less all cash disbursements made during the Foundation's operating history through the report date.

Gift inventory is valued at cost using the First-In, First-Out (FIFO) method. Unit cost is recorded at the time of procurement.

As of the report date, the Foundation has no recorded liabilities. All obligations are settled on a cash basis at the time of disbursement.
