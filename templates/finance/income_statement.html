Notes to Financial Statements

These financial statements have been prepared on the cash basis of accounting. Revenue is recognized when cash is received; expenses are recognized when cash is disbursed.

Donations are recognized as revenue in the period the cash is received. Grants are recognized upon receipt of funds from the funding organization.

The Foundation is a nonprofit organization exempt from income taxes. All net assets are classified as unrestricted unless donor-imposed restrictions exist.
