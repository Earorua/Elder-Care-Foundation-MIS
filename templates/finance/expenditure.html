Notes to Financial Statements

These financial statements have been prepared on the cash basis of accounting. Expenses are recognized when cash is disbursed, not when the obligation is incurred.

Expenses are classified by function: Program Services includes all costs directly related to delivering elder care services (personnel, gifts, delivery). Management & General includes administrative overhead. Currently all expenses are allocated to Program Services as the Foundation does not separately track administrative overhead.

Personnel compensation includes all direct cash payments to staff and volunteers, categorized by payment type (salary, stipend, honorarium, etc.). Payroll taxes and benefits, if any, are included in the respective payment amounts.

Gift procurement costs represent the total cost of goods purchased for distribution to beneficiaries, calculated as batch quantity multiplied by unit cost at time of purchase. Delivery and logistics costs represent actual fees paid to delivery service providers.
