Notes to Financial Statements

These financial statements have been prepared on the cash basis of accounting. Expenses are recognized when cash is disbursed.

Expenses are classified by function: Program Services includes all costs directly related to delivering elder care services. Currently all expenses are allocated to Program Services.

Personnel compensation includes all direct cash payments to staff and volunteers, categorized by payment type.

Gift procurement costs represent the total cost of goods purchased for distribution. Delivery and logistics costs represent actual fees paid to delivery service providers.
